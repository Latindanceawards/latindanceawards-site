    How we score Bachata schools

    We score schools the same way as in Germany, on nine criteria and 100 points: years teaching, beginner offer, events, Google rating, number of reviews and social media (75 points in total), plus festivals, recent reviews and competitions (25 points, currently "to be confirmed"). Data comes from school websites, Google Maps and Instagram. Anything we cannot confirm counts at the lowest value, and a school can fill it in by applying.

    The ranking is based only on public data. Places cannot be bought, and any paid listings are marked as ads and do not affect the points. We collect school data ourselves from Google Maps and school websites, so it may contain mistakes: enter a school or send us a correction.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is the best Bachata school in Ireland?

    Right now, according to our data, Dublin Salsa Academy (Dublin) is in front with 32,7 of 100 points. This is an interim standing; final results follow in spring 2027.

    Where does the data come from?

    From public sources: the rating and review count on Google Maps and the schools’ own websites, where we check that Bachata is taught.

    Can I buy a place in the ranking?

    No. Places cannot be bought and paid listings do not affect the points.

    How do I enter my school?

    Use the free application form. By applying you can confirm your data and get the full score.

    Which cities does the ranking cover?

    To start with: Dublin, Galway, Cork, Limerick, Kilkenny. We add more cities as schools apply.
